# Design references

Each group holds accepted frames a build matches, light and dark. Each line links the group file naming the code it governs.

- [Agents](agents.md): An agent's claim on a frame, its unseen changes, compare, and the offer to jump to a shown frame
- [Canvas](canvas.md): Frames on the canvas, their labels, and selection
- [Foundations](foundations.md): Chrome tokens and parts, the colors, the logo
- [Home](home.md): The project list a board opens on
- [Inspector](inspector.md): The right panel for a frame or an element
- [Landing](landing.md): The landing page in `web/`, a separate project from the board, from the hero through the walkthrough to the install
- [Layers](layers.md): The left panel's pages, layers, and find
- [Menus](menus.md): Right-click and avatar menus, and the copy toast
- [Notes](notes.md): Notes pinned on frames and their threads
- [Palette](palette.md): The command palette and the shortcuts sheet
- [Present](present.md): A page presented full screen one frame at a time, its step bar and slide overview, and the presenter view
- [Shell](shell.md): The board frame around the canvas and its side panels
- [Tabs](tabs.md): The left panel's Theme and Assets tabs
- [Toolbar](toolbar.md): The floating toolbar under the canvas
